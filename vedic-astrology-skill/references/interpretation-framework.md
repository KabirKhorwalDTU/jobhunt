## 2. Planetary Aspects (Drishti)

In Vedic astrology, aspects are different from Western:

### Standard Aspects
All planets aspect the 7th house from their position (180°).

### Special Aspects
- **Mars**: Additionally aspects 4th and 8th houses from its position
- **Jupiter**: Additionally aspects 5th and 9th houses from its position
- **Saturn**: Additionally aspects 3rd and 10th houses from its position
- **Rahu/Ketu**: Same as Jupiter (5th, 7th, 9th) — debated, but widely used

### Aspect Interpretation
- **Benefic aspects** (Jupiter, Venus, well-placed Mercury/Moon): Protect and enhance the aspected house
- **Malefic aspects** (Saturn, Mars, Rahu): Stress, delay, or challenge the aspected house — but also purify and strengthen through adversity
- **Saturn's aspect**: Delays but eventually delivers. Hard work required.
- **Mars's aspect**: Energy, aggression, speed applied to that house
- **Jupiter's aspect**: Protection, expansion, wisdom applied to that house

## 3. Dignity Effects on Interpretation

### Exalted Planet
- Results are amplified and come easily
- The house the planet sits in benefits greatly
- The houses the planet lords over flourish
- Exception: If exalted planet is in a dusthana (6, 8, 12), benefits are mixed — powerful but in a challenging context

### Debilitated Planet
- Results are delayed, reduced, or arrive through struggle
- Neecha Bhanga (cancellation of debilitation) can partially or fully restore: check if the lord of the debilitation sign is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon
- Debilitated planets in the Dasha period bring challenges related to the houses they lord

### Own Sign Planet
- Comfortable, reliable results. The planet acts naturally without strain.
- Like being in your own home — you know the terrain

### Retrograde Planet
- Internal processing of the planet's energy. Effects are felt internally before manifesting externally
- Delays in the planet's significations but eventually deeper results
- Retrograde benefics: benefits come late but are more deeply felt
- Retrograde malefics: challenges are internalized (anxiety, self-doubt rather than external obstacles)

### Combust (within ~6° of Sun)
- Planet's significations are "burned" — hidden or weakened
- The house the combust planet rules suffers reduced visibility
- Mercury and Venus are most commonly combust (close to Sun by nature)

## 4. Nakshatra-Level Refinement

After house and sign analysis, refine using the Nakshatra:

### Nakshatra Groupings
- **Deva (Divine)**: Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati — Sattvic nature, beneficial
- **Manushya (Human)**: Bharani, Rohini, Ardra, Purva Phalguni, Uttara Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Uttara Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada, Uttara Bhadrapada — Mixed nature
- **Rakshasa (Demonic)**: Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Vishakha, Jyeshtha, Moola, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha — Tamasic, intense, transformative

### Key Nakshatra Interpretations for Lagna
The Lagna Nakshatra gives the foundational personality flavor. The Moon Nakshatra (Janma Nakshatra) gives the emotional/mental nature.

## 5. Conjunction & Combination Effects

When planets share the same house (within ~10° for strength):

### Key Conjunctions
- **Sun + Moon**: Amavasya (new moon) near — emotional-ego fusion. Father-mother dynamics important.
- **Sun + Mercury**: Budhaditya Yoga — intelligence and communication enhanced. Combust risk if too close.
- **Jupiter + Rahu**: Guru Chandal Dosh — wisdom confused by illusion. Misleading gurus. Unorthodox beliefs.
- **Jupiter + Moon**: Gajakesari Yoga (if in mutual kendras) — fame, wisdom, lasting reputation.
- **Mars + Saturn**: Intense — discipline meets aggression. Engineering, military. Can be destructive if afflicted.
- **Venus + Mars**: Strong passion, romantic attraction. Can indicate multiple relationships.
- **Saturn + Rahu**: Shrapit Dosh — past-life curse. Delays in the house they occupy. Requires specific remedies.
- **Moon + Rahu**: Grahan Yoga — mind clouded. Anxiety, unusual thinking. Mother may face issues.

### Stellium (3+ planets in one house)
A stellium concentrates life energy in that house's themes. The house becomes dominant in the life narrative.
Multiple planets in one house also leave other houses empty and without direct support.

## 6. Retrograde Planet Interpretation

### Inner Planets (Mercury, Venus)
- More common, less dramatic. Internalized communication (Mercury) or love (Venus).
- Mercury retrograde natal: thinks deeply, may have speech patterns, revises ideas.
- Venus retrograde natal: unusual aesthetic, love comes in non-standard ways.

### Outer Planets (Mars, Jupiter, Saturn)
- Mars retrograde: Anger turned inward. Passive-aggressive. Courage is contemplated rather than acted on.
- Jupiter retrograde: Unconventional wisdom. Questions religion/authority. Internal philosophical growth.
- Saturn retrograde: Karmic lessons revisited. Doesn't accept rules blindly. Restructures from within.

## 7. Divisional Chart Synthesis

### Reading Divisional Charts
1. Note the Lagna of each divisional chart — it shifts the house framework
2. Check where the relevant planets fall in the divisional chart
3. A planet strong in D1 but weak in the relevant Varga: promises made but delivery is compromised
4. A planet weak in D1 but strong in the Varga: hidden strength that emerges in that life area

### D9 Navamsa (Marriage + Dharma)
- **Primary use**: Marriage partner qualities, marriage timing, dharmic path
- Navamsa Lagna sign: The deeper layer of personality, especially after marriage
- 7th house of D9: Partner's nature, marriage quality
- Venus in D9: Quality of love life and marital happiness
- If a planet is Vargottama (same sign in D1 and D9): Its effects are doubled — powerfully good or powerfully challenging

### D10 Dasamsa (Career)
- **Primary use**: Professional life, career type, rise and fall in profession
- D10 Lagna: Career personality (may differ from D1 persona)
- 10th house of D10: The actual nature of the profession
- Sun and Saturn in D10: Authority and discipline in career

### D7 Saptamsa (Children)
- **Primary use**: Number of children, timing, gender, relationship with children
- 5th house of D7: Primary indicator for firstborn
- Jupiter in D7: Blessings regarding children

### D12 Dwadasamsa (Parents + Ancestry)
- **Primary use**: Parents' life, ancestral karma, lineage patterns
- 4th house of D12: Mother's life and health
- 9th house of D12: Father's life and health
- Malefics in D12's 4th or 9th: Health concerns for that parent

### D60 Shastiamsa (Past-Life Karma)
- **Primary use**: Why things happen the way they do — karmic seeds from previous lives
- Each planet falls in one of 60 divisions, each with a named nature
- Benefic D60 names: Deva, Kubera, Amrita, Brahma, Vishnu, etc. — past-life merit
- Malefic D60 names: Ghora, Rakshasa, Mrithyu, Yama, etc. — past-life debt
- A planet in a benefic D60 division: Its positive results come with grace
- A planet in a malefic D60 division: Its area brings karmic lessons and struggle
