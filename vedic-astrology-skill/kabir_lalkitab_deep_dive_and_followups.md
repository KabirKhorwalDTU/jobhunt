**Wake-up remedy:** Saffron tilak daily. Turmeric offering at temple on Thursdays. Study one financial concept every week (a book, a video, a mentor). Jupiter's financial wisdom is latent in you — it needs conscious activation.

**All other planets are AWAKE** — they may be displaced from their Pakka Ghar, but they're actively producing results in their current houses.
