**Sun in "Vishadagdha" (Burnt by Poison)** — In a previous life, you held a position of authority or prominence that was destroyed — possibly through betrayal, political downfall, or misuse of power. **Mars in "Ardra" (Tears/Moisture)** — Mars in a neutral division means your past-life martial/warrior energy was channeled through suffering. You fought, but it cost you tears. **Mercury in "Kulaghna" (Family Destroyer)** — This is heavy. In a past life, your intellect or communication caused family disruption — lies, manipulation, or decisions that broke family bonds. **Jupiter in "Kaala" (Time/Death)** — Jupiter, the planet of wisdom and grace, in the division of "Time/Death" suggests: past-life misuse of spiritual authority. You may have been a teacher, priest, or advisor who misguided others — either intentionally or through ignorance. **Venus in "Bhrashta" (Fallen)** — A past-life fall from grace involving relationships, luxury, or sensuality. ## Karmic Debts (Rin) Identified

### 1. PITRA RIN (Debt to Father / Ancestors)

**Indicator:** Debilitated Sun in Libra.

In Lal Kitab, a weak Sun = your ancestors' blessings are blocked. You may have not honored paternal obligations in a past life. This manifests as: difficulty getting support from father/authority figures, obstacles in government or institutional work, low self-confidence that doesn't match your actual ability, and feeling "unseen" despite doing good work.

**Repayment:** Offer water to the rising Sun every morning (Surya Arghya). Never disrespect your father — even in thought. Feed jaggery (gur) to monkeys or cows on Sundays. Keep a copper coin in your pocket. Never accept free handouts or charity — always insist on paying.

### 2. RAHU RIN (Partnership/Betrayal Karma)
